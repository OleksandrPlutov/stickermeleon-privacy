Privacy Policy

App: StickerMeleon (Android) · Developer: Plutov100 Studio
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the StickerMeleon app (“the app”) handles, why, where it goes and what choices you have. The app is provided by Plutov100 Studio (“we”, “us”).

1. Summary

  • Most of your data stays on your device: your photos, the stickers you make and your settings.
  • Data leaves your device only when you publish a sticker pack to Telegram, when you use Pro subscription features (purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat), when you write to support in the app, and when the app checks that it is a genuine copy (see section 6).
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools of its own. We do not sell your data.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Photos. You choose a photo with the Android system photo picker (no gallery permission is needed) or take one with the camera. The camera permission is requested only when you choose to take a photo. Your original photo is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Background removal. The app removes backgrounds on your device using Google ML Kit. The image is not sent to our servers. The ML Kit model is downloaded by Google Play services, and Google may process technical data (for example usage and diagnostic data about the model) in connection with that service under its own terms.
  • Built-in stickers. The sticker library in the editor is bundled inside the app and is searched on your device. Searching it does not send anything to us or to any third party. See section 12 for attributions.
  • Stickers and packs. The sticker images, pack names, emoji and order are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. You can delete stickers and packs inside the app.
  • Temporary files. Working copies of images are kept in the app’s cache while you edit. The app removes some of them automatically; all of them are removed when you clear the app’s cache or uninstall the app.
  • Settings. Theme, language and notification preferences are stored on your device.
  • Notifications. The app uses local notifications created on your device (reminders and, if you use the support chat, a notice that support has replied). It does not register for push notification services and does not collect a push token. You can turn notifications off in the app’s Settings or in Android settings.

To publish a sticker pack, you first connect your Telegram account to our Telegram bot. Publishing is done by our server (Google Cloud Functions / Firebase, region europe-west1), because the bot’s secret key must not be stored in the app.

The sticker pack itself is stored by Telegram and is publicly accessible through its link. Telegram processes your data under its own privacy policy: telegram.org/privacy.

4. Subscriptions (Sticker Pro)

Pro features are sold as a subscription through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we never see your card or payment details. To check whether a subscription is active, the app uses the service RevenueCat. On every launch the app creates an anonymous RevenueCat identifier and asks for your subscription status. RevenueCat receives that anonymous identifier together with purchase information from Google Play (such as the product, purchase token, dates, price and currency) and basic technical data (such as app and Android version and locale), and may infer your country from your IP address. See revenuecat.com/privacy. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play.

6. App integrity check

To protect our server from abuse, the app proves to the server that it is a genuine copy installed from Google Play. For this the app uses Firebase App Check with the Google Play Integrity API. Google receives information about the app (such as package name, version and signing certificate) and a device integrity signal from Google Play services, and the Firebase SDK adds basic SDK usage information (dates of use, no personal identifiers) to these requests. Our server receives only a short-lived token confirming the result and does not store it. See policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Access code for app reviewers

The app contains a hidden screen where a reviewer (for example from Google Play) can enter an access code to unlock Pro features for 30 days. If you never open this screen, nothing is sent. If the code is entered, the app sends the code and your anonymous RevenueCat identifier to our server, which checks the code and asks RevenueCat to grant a 30-day promotional entitlement to that identifier. A record of the grant (the identifier and expiry date) is kept on our server until it expires.

8. Server logs

Our server records technical error messages. Google Cloud, which hosts the server, automatically keeps standard request logs that include the IP address of the request, the request path and the time. By default they are kept for about 30 days. We use these logs only to keep the service running and to investigate problems.

  • RevenueCat – revenuecat.com/privacy

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: delete stickers and packs inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Telegram link and published-pack records: deleted from our server when you choose Disconnect Telegram in the app. You can also ask us to delete them by email (we will do it within 30 days; please include your Telegram username).
  • Support conversations: deleted with the delete button in the chat, or automatically after 12 months (see section 5). The same 12-month limit applies to Telegram-link records if you choose to disconnect only on the device or the server could not be reached. Copies in the developer’s Telegram chat can be deleted on request.
  • Telegram: you can delete a published pack from the app (“Delete everywhere”).
  • RevenueCat / Google: purchase records are kept by those providers under their own policies. Contact us if you want us to request deletion of your RevenueCat record.

11. Security

Data sent from the app to our server is transmitted over HTTPS. The Telegram link token and the support conversation identifier are stored on your device in Android’s encrypted storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
