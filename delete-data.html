How to request deletion of your data

App: StickerMeleon (Android) · Developer: Plutov100 Studio

StickerMeleon has no user accounts. Most of your data (photos, stickers, packs, settings) stays on your device and is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Only a small amount of data is stored on our server when you connect Telegram and publish a sticker pack. This page explains how to ask us to delete it.

Delete it yourself in the app

  • Telegram link and pack records: in the app, choose Disconnect Telegram. This deletes your records from our server immediately.
  • Support chat: open the support chat and use the delete button. This deletes your conversation from our server immediately.

If you cannot do this in the app, or you also want the copies of your support messages in the developer’s Telegram chat deleted, ask us by email.

Step by step (by email)

  1. Optional, before you disconnect Telegram: in the app, open your sticker pack and choose Delete everywhere to remove published packs from Telegram. (After you disconnect Telegram in the app, packs that were already published stay in Telegram.)
  2. Send an email to plutov100studio@gmail.com with the subject “StickerMeleon data deletion request”.
  3. In the email, write your Telegram username (the one connected to the app) so we can find your records. If you have no username, write the first name shown in the app’s Telegram connection.
  4. We may ask you to confirm that the Telegram account is yours.
  5. We delete the data listed below within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we delete

DataWhere it is storedAction
Telegram numeric user ID, first name, username, link token and link timeOur database (Google Cloud Firestore)Deleted
Records of sticker packs you published (pack name, title, number of stickers, creation time)Our database (Google Cloud Firestore)Deleted

We do not store your photos or sticker images on our server, so there is nothing to delete there.

What we keep or cannot delete ourselves

DataWhy
Sticker packs already published in TelegramThey are stored by Telegram. You can remove them with Delete everywhere in the app (step 1). Contact us if you cannot, and we will try to help.
Purchase records (Google Play and RevenueCat)They are held by Google and by RevenueCat under their own policies, and may be kept for tax, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons. If you want us to request deletion from RevenueCat, include your Google Play order number from your purchase receipt in your email. We will make the request where the record can be identified.
Technical server logsKept by Google Cloud for about 30 days under its default settings (they include the IP address of requests) and not tied to your profile; they cannot be deleted individually.

Data on your device

We cannot access data stored on your phone. To remove it, delete your stickers and packs in the app, or uninstall StickerMeleon.
